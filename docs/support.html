Support

Parla turns voice messages into text, entirely on your device.

Something broken, or an idea? Open an issue at github.com/OleksandrUskov/Parla/issues. It is the fastest way to reach me, and the whole app's source is there too.

Common questions

Why does it download something on first run?
The speech-recognition model. It is downloaded once, then transcription runs fully offline. You can pick a smaller, faster model instead of the recommended one, or skip the download and do it later.
The first transcription took much longer than the ones after it.
That is expected the first time each model is used on a device: the system compiles it for the Neural Engine, which takes several minutes for the recommended model. Later runs reuse the compiled version and start in seconds.
Does Parla upload my audio?
No. There is no upload path in the app. See the privacy policy.
Which languages does it handle?
Whisper's full language set, with particular care taken over Russian and Ukrainian. The interface itself is English, Russian and Ukrainian, and a fresh install opens in your system's language when it is one of those.
Which files can it read?
Telegram voice messages (.ogg, .oga, .opus), plus .m4a, .mp3, .wav, .aac, .flac, .amr, .aiff, .aif, .caf, and the video containers .mp4, .mov, .m4v and .3gp (the audio track is used). WebM and Matroska are not supported. A shared file with no extension is recognized by its content, so Telegram voice messages saved without an extension still work.
I bought Pro in the App Store version and it is not showing up.
Use Restore purchase on the upgrade screen or in Settings. A purchase is tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in with the one you bought it with.
What is free and what is paid in the App Store version?
Free covers unlimited notes up to five minutes each, dictation, automatic titles, keywords and summaries, and TXT/Markdown export. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the length limit and adds chapters for long recordings, on-device generative summaries, batch processing (plus folder drops and folder watching on the Mac), and SRT/VTT/JSON export.
Where are my transcripts stored?
In the app's own storage on your device, as plain files. Deleting a note removes its text and its audio copy. Deleting the app removes everything on iPhone and iPad; on a Mac, see the privacy policy.
