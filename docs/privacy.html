Privacy Policy

Parla — last updated 19 August 2026

Parla collects nothing. There is no account, no server we operate, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you record, import or write stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Parla does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK that receives anything about you.

On-device processing

Transcription, dictation and text analysis all run entirely on your device, using Apple's on-device machine-learning frameworks. Your audio and your transcripts are never uploaded. There is no upload path in the app at all.

Network use

Parla uses the network only to download, never to send:

  • the speech-recognition model, once, on first run — after which transcription works fully offline;
  • the optional generative and text-embedding models, only if you choose to enable those features.

These requests fetch public model files and contain no personal information about you. Purchases are handled by Apple's StoreKit, which communicates with Apple — not with us; we never see your payment details and receive no personal data from a purchase.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only while you are actively dictating, to turn your speech into text on your device. The recording is not sent anywhere. Whether an audio copy is kept on your device is your choice in Settings, and you can delete it at any time.

Files you open

When you drop in or share an audio or video file, Parla reads it to transcribe it. It is not copied anywhere outside your device, and Parla asks the system for access to nothing beyond the files you hand it and any folder you explicitly choose to watch.

Your data, and deleting it

Your transcripts live in Parla's own storage on your device. Deleting a note removes its text and its audio copy; deleting the app removes everything. Since nothing was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Parla collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else: open an issue at github.com/OleksandrUskov/Parla/issues.
