Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Parla collects nothing. There is no account, no server we operate, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you record, import or write is uploaded by Parla.

Data we collect

None. Parla does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it never uploads your recordings, transcripts or notes. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no SDK that collects data about you. Speech and optional AI models come from Hugging Face, which sees your IP address as any website does. If you have signed in to Hugging Face with its own tools on your Mac, the download library sends your saved access token with model requests.

On-device processing

Transcription, dictation and text analysis all run entirely on your device, using Apple's on-device machine-learning frameworks. Parla never uploads your audio or your transcripts. There is no upload path in the app at all.

Network use

Parla uses the network to download, and it never uploads your content:

  • the list of available speech models, each time the app starts;
  • the speech-recognition model you pick, once per model, after which transcription works fully offline;
  • Apple's language assets for on-device text analysis, which the system downloads from Apple once if your device does not have them yet;
  • the optional generative and text-embedding models, only if you choose to enable those features.

These requests fetch public model files and include nothing from your recordings or notes. Purchases are handled by Apple's StoreKit, which communicates with Apple and not with us; we never see your payment details and receive no personal data from a purchase.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only while you are actively dictating, to turn your speech into text on your device. Parla does not send the recording anywhere. Whether an audio copy is kept on your device is your choice in Settings, and you can delete it at any time.

Files you open

When you drop in or share an audio or video file, Parla reads it to transcribe it. Parla does not copy it anywhere outside your device. Parla does not browse your files: it reads the ones you give it and any folder you choose to watch.

Your data, and deleting it

Your transcripts live in Parla's own storage on your device. Deleting a note removes its text and its audio copy. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes everything it stored. On a Mac, moving Parla to the Trash leaves its data behind. To remove everything, also delete ~/Library/Application Support/com.parla.app (~/Library/Application Support/Parla for v1.0.0), ~/Library/Containers/com.parla.app, ~/Library/Caches/com.parla.app, ~/Library/HTTPStorages/com.parla.app and ~/Library/Preferences/com.parla.app.plist, and the argmaxinc and openai folders in ~/Documents/huggingface/models if no other app keeps its models there. Since Parla never sent your data to us, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Parla collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact
