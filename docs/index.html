Parla

Voice messages, audio and video turned into text — entirely on your Mac or iPhone. No cloud, no account, nothing uploaded.

What it does

  • Reads Telegram voice messages (.ogg/.opus) and the usual audio and video formats.
  • Transcribes on-device with Whisper, so it works offline and your recordings stay yours.
  • Writes a title, keywords and a summary for each note — also on-device.
  • Dictation with a global hotkey on the Mac, and a share sheet on the iPhone.
  • Long recordings are split into chapters you can jump around in.

Privacy, briefly

There is no upload path in the app. The network is used only to download the recognition model once, and it never sends anything. The full policy is one short page.
